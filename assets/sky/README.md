# Overcast sky photography

`overcast-soil-4k.jpg` is the 4096 × 2048 game derivative of Poly Haven's
[Overcast Soil (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/overcast_soil_puresky).
Original photography: Sergej Majboroda. Sky edits: Jarod Guest.
License: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

Source: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/extra/Tonemapped%20JPG/overcast_soil_puresky.jpg

The source tonemapped panorama was resized with bicubic resampling and saved
as JPEG quality 90. The game ships this image locally and samples it on its
camera-centered sky dome. Its display colors are decoded to linear once,
then rendered through the same exposure and tone mapping as the street.
